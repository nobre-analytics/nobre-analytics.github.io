Projects

Four case studies from a collections and payments operation. Each one shows the problem, my part in the work, how the solution works, what changed and what still depends on people.

Core case studies

Case 01Measured from records

How the morning routine stopped depending on manual triggers

Problem
The dialer list had to be ready before 8:00, but each step waited for someone to trigger the previous one.
My part
I measured the waits between steps and set up a sequence that starts on its own at 6:00, with a person monitoring failures.
Result
List ready between 06:22 and 06:57 in the 3 records after the change; before, a 07:30 median over 29 days.

Case 02Qualitative result

How I organised and partly automated the analysis of rejected payments

Problem
Where each rejected payment went depended on who analysed it, and the same case could be handled in different ways.
My part
I gathered the rules with the team and built the extraction, the rules that suggest the next step and the working panel.
Result
A recorded reason for every case; anything without enough evidence goes to a person for review.
Read the case →See the demo
Case 03Qualitative result

How I started identifying duplicate payments before any refund

Problem
A customer who pays the same instalment twice must get the money back, but only once the duplicate is proven.
My part
I designed the identification rules and built a panel that separates who builds the batch from who carries out the refund.
Result
Status and history for every case in one place. The decision and the payment stay with people.
Read the case →See the demo
Case 04Qualitative result

How I made failures in the pre-workday routines visible

Problem
When an overnight routine failed without anyone noticing, the team started the day with incomplete data.
My part
I built a screen that shows whether the morning closed and where a failure stopped, and fixed a bug that displayed wrong values.
Result
Failures visible with the step where they stopped. Includes a version for the manager’s phone.
