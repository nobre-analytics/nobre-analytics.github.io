Projects

Four case studies from a collections and payments operation. Each one shows the problem, my responsibility, how the solution works and the type of evidence available.

Core case studies

  1. 01 Morning Operations Routine Automation A scheduled sequence instead of chained manual triggers, so the dialer list is ready before operations start. Evidence: recorded times · 29 days before, 3 days after
  2. 02 Rejected Payments Decision Engine A rules engine that classifies each rejection, records the reason and sends exceptions to review. Evidence: rules and demo · qualitative result
  3. 03 Duplicate Payment Controls Finds and organizes duplicate payments for human refund review, without moving money. Evidence: controls and demo · no amounts published
  4. 04 Operations Monitor Status of automated routines, failures, retries and hand-off to a person. Evidence: implemented features and demo · qualitative result
  5. 04+ Operations Monitor: mobile version A UX extension of case 04: the morning verdict first, details on demand. Not counted as a separate project

Other initiative

  1. ·Duplicate write-off reconcilerA Python bot that handles payments written off twice in the collections system. Partially executed; a different project from the duplicate payment controls.
