Extension of the Operations Monitor · Mobile UX

Operations Monitor: mobile version

The same monitor, redesigned for the phone. The morning verdict comes first, and the detail is one tap away.

Designed for the managerTouch screenLight and dark theme

The challenge

The manager wants the morning numbers before getting to a computer.

On desktop, the monitor shows every workstream at once. On a phone, that much information turns into endless scrolling. The question changed: instead of "what is happening everywhere?", it became "do I need to act now?". The expected benefit, not yet measured, is that managers know within seconds, without a computer, whether they need to act.

Desktop and mobile

Not the same screen made smaller. A different hierarchy.

Desktop: every workstream side by side, for analysis.

Mobile: the verdict first.

Design decisions

Three choices shaped the mobile version.

  • 1Verdict first

    The first thing on the screen answers whether the morning closed.

  • 2Detail on demand

    Tables become lists, and the detail opens in a sheet that slides up from the bottom.

  • 3Navigation within thumb reach

    The menu sits at the bottom of the screen. In the demo, notes beside the phone explain each change.

On a computer, the demo shows the phone with notes on each change. On a phone, it fills the whole screen.
