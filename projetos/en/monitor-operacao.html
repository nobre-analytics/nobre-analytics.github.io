Operations Monitor

A monitor for the fleet of data bots. In seconds, it shows whether the morning closed, how much came in and what failed.

The challenge

A failure during the night has to show up before the team starts.

The operation depends on bots that run before the workday. If one of them fails and nobody notices, the team starts the day working with incomplete data. The problem was not only running the bots, it was knowing, fast, whether the morning was ready.

The approach

I built the screen around the three questions the manager asks every morning.

Did the morning close?

Every bot, task and source, with the time its data was saved.

How much came in?

Collections by channel and by days past due.

What failed?

The failed task opens a detail view that shows the step where it stopped.

The solution

One screen for the verdict, the detail one click away.

  • Status of every bot, task and source, with the time of the last save.
  • Detail drawer that opens the failed task and shows the step that stopped.
  • Quick navigation between sections with CtrlK.
  • Remote access in the browser, with nothing to install on the viewer's computer.

A matter of trust

When the numbers are wrong, nobody comes back to the panel.

Symptom

Payment and sequence values were sometimes shown wrong.

Root cause

A race condition: two updates were competing for the same data.

Fix

I fixed the order of the updates so the panel shows the right value.
