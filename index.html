How I work

I turn operational problems into structured, measurable, and sustainable solutions.

This is how I run an initiative, from the first sign of a problem to a result the operation can keep without me.

01

Diagnose

Find the real problem first.

I map the process, trace the data and look for the root cause. I size the impact and the risk, so the initiative targets what really needs to change.

02

Structure

Turn the problem into an initiative.

I define scope, priorities, owners, stakeholders, dependencies and success criteria, and agree on how we will deliver.

03

Execute

Coordinate and remove blockers.

I coordinate Operations, Technology and management, track dependencies, escalate when needed and keep everyone clear on status.

04

Sustain

Make the result last.

I measure the impact, document the process, set controls and transfer knowledge, so the result does not depend on me. Then I look for where it can scale.

My view

Technology is the means. The result is a better process.

I automate to reduce dependency, risk and manual effort.

Every project needs an owner, a direction and a result you can measure.

Well-structured processes make results predictable and sustainable.

My Story

From operations to delivery

I understand operations because I started there. At Itaú Unibanco I worked in Customer Due Diligence, Collateral Management and internal people consulting, always with high-volume processes, data and KPIs.

There I learned to look at the process before the tool.

At Simplic, the work grew from process improvement into project delivery: diagnosing problems, structuring initiatives, connecting Operations and Technology, and measuring the result. Automation is part of the toolkit, used to remove manual dependency and risk from the operation.

My next step is to apply this experience in environments of greater scale and complexity, contributing to initiatives in process improvement, operations, project delivery and automation. I am looking for opportunities where I can connect strategy and execution, turn operational problems into structured solutions and deliver measurable results, including in international and remote teams.
