How he works

Problem first, tools second

Automation is a means, not the goal. This is how Paulo runs a product, from problem to result.

01

Discover

Find the real problem first.

He maps the flow with the people who run it and measures where time, money and control are lost, before proposing any solution.

02

Define rules and requirements

What lives in heads becomes rules.

He turns what lives in people’s heads into explicit rules and clear requirements, with the exceptions and who decides each one.

03

Prioritise

Customer impact first.

He prioritises by customer impact, financial and operational risk, volume and capacity, and makes trade-offs explicit. With management aligned, he accepted that five short-SLA cases would miss their deadline in order to finish the automation that now handles more than 200 rejections a day.

04

Deliver and measure

The result has to last.

He automates only what always follows the same rule, records what changed with the period and sample, and defines what happens when something fails.

What he believes

Technology is the means. The result is a better process.

Automation exists to reduce dependency, risk and manual effort.

Every product needs an owner, a direction and a result you can measure.

Well-structured processes make results predictable and sustainable.

Background

From operations to delivery

Paulo understands operations because he started there. At Itaú Unibanco he worked in Customer Due Diligence, notarial guarantees and internal people consulting, always with high-volume processes, data and KPIs.

There he learned to look at the process before the tool.

Today, at a credit fintech, he leads internal products from diagnosis to result: he understands the problem with the people who run it, defines the rules, prioritises with management and delivers a working solution. Automation is a means to remove manual dependency and risk from the operation.

His next step is to work as a Product Owner of internal and operational products at fintechs, banks and technology companies, including international and remote teams.
